Summary for Search: The Upsilon Phi Sigma 1935 Aborlan Municipal Executive Council (Aborlan MEC) is the municipal council of Upsilon Phi Sigma in Aborlan, Palawan, Philippines. Its collegiate chapter is the Upsilon Gamma Iota Chapter at Western Philippines University Main Campus. It was pioneered in June 2017 by four pioneers — Geofrelle Angel, Mark John Labra, Mark James Corla, and Lurman Rey Manlangit — and has since led community service, sports, disaster relief, environmental, and youth programs in Aborlan.

1. The National History: Upsilon Phi Sigma 1935

Upsilon Phi Sigma (ΥΦΣ) was founded on February 14, 1935 — Valentine's Day — at the University of the Philippines Los Baños. The date was chosen deliberately to emphasize love and peace.

Seven students from different colleges organized the fraternity. Its constitution was finalized on May 14, 1935. Its motto is UNITAS PAX ET SALUS — Unity, Peace, and Welfare for All. Its pillars are Honor, Service, and Excellence.

Originally a men-only fraternity, Upsilon Phi Sigma later became a fraternity and sorority. Today it has more than 160 chapters, with active chapters in the Philippines and international chapters in Australia, New Zealand, USA, Canada, Germany, Taiwan, and Saudi Arabia. On February 14, 2026, it celebrated its 91st Founding Anniversary.

2. The Pioneers in Aborlan

In the quiet town of Aborlan, particularly in Barangay Isaub, four close friends bonded through shared experiences as students at Western Philippines University (WPU) Main Campus. Beyond academics, they dreamt of making a lasting mark on their school.

In June 2017, they made a bold decision: rather than joining well-established fraternities, they would establish a new chapter of Upsilon Phi Sigma 1935, a fraternity that had yet to reach their campus.

6. What is Aborlan MEC? What is Upsilon Gamma Iota?

Upsilon Phi Sigma (National) → Palawan Provincial Executive Council → Aborlan Municipal Executive Council (MEC) → Upsilon Gamma Iota Chapter (WPU Main).

The MEC is the municipal council — it manages all UPS members, the chapter, and alumni in the whole municipality of Aborlan. The Upsilon Gamma Iota Chapter is the collegiate chapter based at WPU Main Campus, Aborlan. Its full name in publications is: UPS WPU-MAIN Upsilon Gamma Iota Chapter.

7. Leadership: Chairman Geofrelle “Geo” Ang

Under the leadership of Chairman Geofrelle “Geo” Ang, the Aborlan MEC earned recognition from the Barangay Council and SK Council for “pagbibigay kulay ng ating Barangay” — literally giving color to the barangay through repainting, court rehabilitation, and youth programs. The Barangay and SK Council publicly thanked UPS 1935 led by Chairman Geo Ang and his companions.

Chairman Geo Ang also represented Aborlan at the 8th UPS Mindanao Summit, showing that Aborlan MEC had grown from a kubo in Isaub to a nationally recognized council.

8. Community Service and Legacy Projects

8.1 Waiting Shed Project

A green waiting shed with UPS 1935 logo at the public market area — a lasting public facility for commuters in Aborlan.

8.2 Road Safety: “Slow Down, Children Crossing”

Yellow road signage installed by UPS Aborlan MEC for the safety of children crossing — a project for the whole community, not just members.

8.3 Basketball Tournament and Court Rehabilitation

Organizing and sponsoring the Inter-Barangay Basketball Tournament in Barangay Isaub, including repainting and rehabilitating the basketball court by members themselves.

8.4 Maligaya Sports and Youth Festival 2024

On May 28, 2024, UPS Aborlan MEC received a Certificate of Appreciation from Barangay Iraan for invaluable contribution and support during the Maligaya Sports and Youth Festival 2024, signed by SK Chairperson Hon. Reyna Michelle Ruhen and Barangay Captain Hon. Neywin D. Basi.

8.5 One Day Futsal League 2024, Isaub

On December 30, 2024, Upsilon Gamma Iota Chapter received a Certificate of Recognition and Appreciation for supporting the One Day Futsal League at Isaub, signed by Isaub Football Club Tournament Manager Daniel John G. Salazar and President Charles Earl C. Temple.

8.6 Flood Relief — House-to-House

Members walked through floodwaters to deliver food packs and relief packs house-to-house, including to mothers with children and families in need. The banner carried was “Upsilon Phi Sigma Palawan — Honor and Excellence Fraternity and Sorority Since 1935.”

8.7 Feeding Program — Libreng Lugaw

Members cooked in large pots and served free lugaw in cups to children and residents during community events, including the Color Fun Run.

8.8 Color Fun Run + Coastal Clean-Up + Tree Planting — August 9, 2025

Theme: “Takbo Kontra Droga, Kulayan ang Pag-asa”. Assembly: Marikit Evacuation Center, 5:30 AM. The event combined: (1) Color Fun Run for youth, (2) Anti-drug advocacy, (3) Coastal Clean-Up, (4) Tree Planting, (5) Free Lugaw for participating children. Conducted in partnership with PNP and the local community.

8.9 Harvest Festival — Sitio Mayligan, Barangay Iraan — May 25-27, 2025

Members participated in and supported the Harvest Festival, joining the community celebration.

9. National Recognition: Social Media Champ

At the 9th Upsilon Phi Sigma National Convention, May 28-30, 2025, the Aborlan Municipal Executive Council received the Gawad Parangal ng Pamamuno (Social Media Champ) award. This recognized Aborlan MEC’s consistent documentation of its activities — from founding stories to relief operations — which helped the history of UPS Aborlan reach a national audience.

10. Legacy

What began as a dream shared by four friends in Barangay Isaub has become a lasting legacy. Today, the Upsilon Gamma Iota Chapter and the Aborlan MEC continue to thrive at WPU Aborlan, guided by brotherhood, service, and leadership — attracting students from diverse backgrounds, united by commitment to fraternity ideals.
